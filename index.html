The state of outbound

Outbound is harder than it's ever been

<1%cold email reply rates
<4%cold call pickup rates

It's never been easier for buyers to ignore you and your entire sales org. Your cold calls go straight to voicemail (or AI gatekeepers). And your cold emails end up in spam.

The irony is oh so painful...the sales engagement tools (Outreach, Salesloft, etc) designed to make outbound easier have made it exponentially harder. And it feels impossible to get the attention of our buyers right now.

The game has fundamentally changed. Not incrementally. Fundamentally.

What stopped working

The old way doesn't work anymore

Predictable Revenue is dead

It doesn't work anymore for SDRs to do all the outbound for Account Executives. Everyone needs to own pipeline generation.

Mass blast is over

Outbound has traditionally been a volume sport. That approach died. You must master 1:1 conversations with buyers to secure meetings.

Buyers don't want to meet

75% of buyers prefer a rep-free buying experience (Gartner). We must lead with offers that give buyers value in first meetings, not pitches.

We're in a new era

Here's what's working right now

Our approach is battle-tested with 250+ sales teams. And our studies across 20,000+ reps show that top reps book 5-8x more qualified meetings than average reps. We built a simple methodology called Too Good To Ignore to help you apply these learnings.

Disqualify

We must ruthlessly disqualify opportunities unworthy of our energy, time, and attention.

Offer

We must be consultative and make strategic "gives" to buyers early and often to earn their time.

Provoke

We must engage buyers with a strong point of view on their problem that compels action now.
